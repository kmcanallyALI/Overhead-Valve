1. Safety & Job Setup

This module refreshes the DAF MX-13 overhead valve clearance check/adjust procedure. It's built for techs who've already run this job — it's a certification refresher, not a first-time walkthrough.

Before commencing any activity on the vehicle:
  • Always remove the ignition key and secure it in a safe location. This prevents unintentional starting of the vehicle and activation of electrical systems.
  • When working on a component with direct power supply (+30), always disconnect the 12V battery's ground connection per the applicable job procedure.
  • Dirt or debris entering the engine damages the engine or may cause engine failure — clean the entire engine or engine compartment where service will be performed.

3. Check Valve Clearance

Step 1 — Position the crankshaft

  1. Use special tool 1903018 to turn the crankshaft counterclockwise as seen from the distribution side. This matches the engine's normal direction of rotation.
  2. Crank the engine until the vibration damper is on cylinder mark 1/6. Piston position can also be read on the flywheel.
3
CRANK!
Turning the crankshaft with the ST1903018 tool to align the flywheel mark.

Step 3 — Measure valve clearance

A valve clearance of more than 1 mm [0.04 in] indicates mechanical damage or wear in the valve train — especially if found on only one cylinder. Check all valve train parts on that cylinder before adjusting to spec.

Intake: Measure between the Valve Bridge and the Flat Face of the Intake rocker's Ball Socket.

Exhaust: Insert special tool 2124332 between the bottom surface of the rocker and the upper surface of the lost motion shaft's collar. The tool is designed to straddle the shaft.

  • Insert the tool, then remove it and wipe off any residual oil. Insert it again to take the actual measurement.
  • Residual oil on the lost motion shaft affects the tool's "drag" friction, which will throw off the adjustment.
5
SLIDE...
Inserting the ST2124332 tool and sliding it to feel clearance at the lost motion shaft collar.

4. Adjust Valve Clearance

  1. Adjust the valve clearance by loosening the lock nut and turning the adjusting screw in the correct direction.
  2. Rotate the crankshaft 1/3 stroke at a time (special tool 1903018) to move through the remaining cylinders, following the injection sequence 1‑5‑3‑6‑2‑4.
6
TURN!
Loosening the lock nut and turning the adjusting screw to bring clearance to spec.

5. Gasket & Valve Cover Reinstall

Mount valve cover gasket

  1. Clean the sealing surface of the valve train casing and the recess in the valve cover.
  2. Always install a new gasket in the valve cover — make sure the sealing surface and recess are fully dry to prevent leakage.

Cleaning the old gasket channel and sealing surface before fitting the new one.
8

Install valve cover

  1. Fit the valve cover complete with the attachment bolts and the new valve cover gasket.
  2. Tighten the attachment bolts in the specified sequence to the specified torque.
